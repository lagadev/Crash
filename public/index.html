About the game

Each round starts with a 5 second betting window. Once it begins, a rocket climbs and the multiplier rises with it. Cash out any time before the rocket crashes to lock in bet × multiplier Stars. Wait too long and the round crashes — any bet still in play is lost.

Every round's crash point is generated from a provably-fair seed: its hash is published before the round starts, so results can be independently verified afterwards.
